Common Questions

Fire Alarm & Electrical Service FAQs

Straight answers about fire alarm inspections, compliance, and what it's like to work with Henson Fire.

How often does a commercial fire alarm system need to be inspected in Massachusetts?+
Under NFPA 72, commercial fire alarm systems require testing at set intervals — typically monthly, semi-annually, or annually depending on the component — performed by licensed technicians. Henson Fire schedules and documents every required inspection so your property stays compliant with Massachusetts fire code year-round.
What does a fire alarm inspection include?+
A full inspection tests all manual pull stations, smoke and heat detectors, notification appliances (horns and strobes), the annunciator panel, the main fire alarm panel, and all associated communication equipment to confirm the system will function correctly in an emergency.
Does Henson Fire offer 24-hour emergency fire alarm service?+
Yes. Henson Fire's licensed technicians are on call 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays, for emergency fire alarm repairs and service calls throughout Medford and the Greater Boston area.
Is Henson Fire a licensed fire protection contractor?+
Yes. Henson Fire is an actively licensed fire protection contractor in Massachusetts, founded by a family with more than 100 years of combined firefighting experience.
What areas does Henson Fire serve?+
Henson Fire serves Medford and surrounding Greater Boston communities, including Somerville, Malden, Melrose, Arlington, Cambridge, Everett, Winchester, Stoneham, Woburn, and Boston, providing fire alarm and electrical services to commercial and industrial properties.
What's the difference between fire alarm monitoring and fire alarm inspection?+
Monitoring is continuous, 24/7 central station oversight that alerts emergency responders the moment your system detects an issue. Inspection is a scheduled, hands-on test of your equipment to confirm it's working properly and meets NFPA 72 and local code requirements. Henson Fire provides both.
